# Note sheet (phone)

**Mode:** Operate. Jot a Note mid-read, keyboard up at once.

## Direction
Raised sheet over a soft ink shade, 12px top corners, hairline top edge, lifted shadow turned upward. "Note on *Title*" with a quiet Close; the Note in 17px serif with no box; "Add a quote" / "Add a page" as small-caps quiet links; a full-width italic ink "Save note".

## Memorable moment
On save, the Note's red numeral inks in on the Book screen (#140).

## Constraints
Rides the visual viewport over the keyboard; the draft survives close, back and refresh.
